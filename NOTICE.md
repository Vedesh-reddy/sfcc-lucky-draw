# Attribution and terms

This repository contains the `plugin_luckydraw` and `bm_luckydraw` cartridges developed by Vedesh Reddy for Salesforce Storefront Reference Architecture (SFRA) storefronts. They are an SFRA extension, not an official Salesforce product.

The storefront cartridge overrides or wraps SFRA templates and modules (listed in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)) with `module.superModule`, and uses SFRA's `server` module, middleware and helpers. The applicable Salesforce terms are preserved in [SFRA-TERMS.txt](licenses/SFRA-TERMS.txt).
